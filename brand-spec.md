# Brand specification — عيادة الدكتورة فايزة الوصابي

## Brand role

موقع عيادة نسائية وتوليد في ذمار. النبرة هادئة، واضحة، محترمة، ومطمئنة دون وعود علاجية أو مبالغات تسويقية.

## Core visual tokens

- Deep teal: `#0B4F5C`
- Action green: `#167565`
- Ivory background: `#FBFAF7`
- Pale blush: `#F7E8EA`
- Body ink: `#17323A`
- Muted text: `#5D7378`
- Arabic typeface: IBM Plex Sans Arabic
- Spacing base: 8px
- Radius: 12px controls, 18–23px cards and sections

## Recognizable assets

- Primary logo: `website/assets/logo-horizontal-primary.png`

## Generated editorial assets

These are generic editorial images generated for visual support. They are not photos of the named doctor, clinic, staff, or patients and must not be presented as such.

- Hero: `website/assets/generated/hero-mother-care.webp`
- Pregnancy article: `website/assets/generated/pregnancy-care.webp`
- Women's consultation article: `website/assets/generated/womens-consultation.webp`
- Fertility article: `website/assets/generated/fertility-support.webp`

## Content guardrails

- Do not publish patient images, testimonials, ratings, exact procedure availability, map pins, or social links without confirmation and appropriate permission.
- Keep 24-hour wording limited to reception of childbirth cases, not all services or general emergency care.
- Medical articles are general education and must not imply diagnosis or individualized treatment.
- Preserve the official clinic name, phone number, address, regular hours, and professional title from `docs/CLINIC_MASTER.md`.
